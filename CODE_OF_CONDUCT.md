# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Universal IR to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the community and its users;
- showing empathy and kindness toward others.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private;
- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances;
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission;
- trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour;
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the contact options at <https://abedallask.netlify.app/>. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate.
